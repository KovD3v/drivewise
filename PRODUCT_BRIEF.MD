# Drivewise Product Brief

## What the product is

Drivewise is an agent-based vehicle companion that helps people make informed automotive decisions throughout the complete vehicle lifecycle.

It begins before purchase by helping users understand their needs, evaluate vehicles, compare exact variants and offers, and recognize important trade-offs. After purchase, it continues supporting the owner with relevant updates, vehicle history, maintenance information, known issues, recalls, regulatory changes, upgrades, and eventual resale or replacement decisions.

Drivewise is not simply a search engine for vehicle listings. Listings and external information are evidence used by the product's advisor to educate the user and support better decisions.

Its central promise is:

> Drivewise understands the person, knows the vehicle, explains the evidence at the right level, and remains useful throughout ownership.

## Who it is for

Drivewise is for current and prospective vehicle owners with different levels of automotive knowledge.

This includes:

- First-time buyers who need concepts explained clearly.
- Buyers comparing vehicles, variants, and real offers.
- Owners who want to stay informed about their vehicle.
- Enthusiasts researching maintenance, modifications, or upgrades.
- People preparing to sell, replace, or reassess a vehicle.

The experience must adapt to each user. It should be equally capable of answering "What is a transmission?" and helping an experienced owner evaluate an exhaust upgrade, including compatibility, expected effects, legal constraints, cost, and trade-offs.

The initial validation audience is private vehicle buyers and owners in Italy. The underlying product concept may later support additional markets.

## Which problem it solves

Vehicle information is fragmented, technical, inconsistent, and often designed to generate a sale rather than support an informed decision.

Before purchasing, users must combine listings, specifications, reviews, ownership reports, pricing information, safety data, and mechanical knowledge. They may not know which facts matter, whether two sources refer to the same variant, or how a vehicle fits their actual needs.

After purchasing, this research usually stops. Owners must independently discover recalls, known faults, maintenance requirements, regulatory changes, relevant news, upgrade implications, and changes affecting the vehicle's value or usability.

Drivewise solves this fragmentation by maintaining context about the user and vehicle, gathering attributable information, explaining its significance, and turning it into appropriate actions throughout the vehicle lifecycle.

## Main features and capabilities

### Adaptive agent-based advisor

The advisor learns the user's goals, constraints, vehicle knowledge, preferences, and current lifecycle stage. It adjusts terminology and depth without removing important details.

It can:

- Explain automotive concepts.
- Translate user needs into vehicle requirements.
- Compare vehicles, variants, and offers.
- Present benefits, compromises, risks, and uncertainty.
- Recommend concrete next steps.
- Retain relevant context across the ownership journey.

### Informed purchase guidance

Drivewise guides the user from initial research to a defensible purchase decision.

Capabilities include:

- Needs and usage assessment.
- Budget-aware vehicle discovery.
- Exact model and variant identification.
- Offer-to-variant matching.
- Evidence-backed comparisons.
- Ownership-cost and suitability analysis.
- Known-issue and red-flag identification.
- Inspection and purchase checklists.
- Shortlists with transparent reasoning.

Listings support this process, but they do not replace guidance or explanation.

### Persistent vehicle profile

Each relevant vehicle has a persistent profile connecting its identity and configuration with the user's relationship to it.

A vehicle may be:

- Researched.
- Compared.
- Shortlisted.
- Inspected.
- Purchased.
- Owned.
- Maintained.
- Modified.
- Sold or replaced.

The profile provides continuity between decisions instead of treating each interaction as an isolated search.

### Ownership monitoring and notifications

After purchase, Drivewise continues monitoring information relevant to the owned vehicle.

Updates may include:

- Recalls and safety notices.
- Known mechanical or software issues.
- Manufacturer announcements.
- Maintenance requirements.
- Regulatory and legal changes.
- Model-specific news.
- Market-value developments.
- Compatibility or availability of parts and upgrades.

Notifications should be relevant, timely, attributable, and actionable—not a generic automotive news feed.

### Agent-based information gathering

Information-gathering agents collect and reconcile data from authorized sources.

The system should:

- Track source, publication date, and retrieval date.
- Distinguish exact vehicle facts from general model information.
- Separate verified facts, estimates, opinions, and inferences.
- Detect conflicting or stale information.
- Communicate missing evidence and uncertainty.
- Prevent unreviewed or unauthorized information from silently affecting recommendations.

Scraping is an implementation mechanism, not permission to reuse any available content. Source authorization, provenance, and review remain mandatory.

### Maintenance and upgrade guidance

Owners can research maintenance, repairs, accessories, and modifications in the context of their exact vehicle.

Guidance should consider:

- Compatibility.
- Intended benefit.
- Cost and complexity.
- Reliability and warranty implications.
- Safety and emissions consequences.
- Local legal or homologation requirements.
- Reversibility and effect on resale value.

Drivewise informs decisions; it does not replace qualified mechanical inspection or professional installation.

## Project scope

### Included

The product vision includes:

- Pre-purchase education and decision support.
- Vehicle, variant, and offer comparison.
- Adaptive explanations for different knowledge levels.
- Persistent user and vehicle context.
- Full vehicle-lifecycle support.
- Relevant ownership monitoring and notifications.
- Agent-based advisory interactions.
- Authorized, attributable information gathering.
- Maintenance, issue, recall, and upgrade research.
- Transparent reasoning, provenance, assumptions, and uncertainty.
- Purchase, ownership, resale, and replacement decision support.

The initial product stage concentrates on establishing trustworthy vehicle identity, evidence-backed purchase guidance, and the persistent foundation needed for later ownership features.

### Excluded

Drivewise does not initially include:

- Purchasing vehicles on the user's behalf.
- Acting as a dealer or classified-listing marketplace.
- Paid placement influencing recommendations.
- Financing, insurance, tax, legal, mechanical, or investment guarantees.
- Automatic mechanical diagnosis without appropriate evidence.
- Performing repairs or modifications.
- Collecting or republishing data without source permission.
- Presenting scraped or unreviewed information as verified fact.
- Fully autonomous actions with financial, legal, or safety consequences.
- Broad public launch before data quality and trust requirements are satisfied.

Drivewise may explain professional advice, help users prepare questions, and identify when an expert is needed, but it must not pretend to replace that expert.

## Goals that define success

### Purchase outcomes

Drivewise succeeds when users can:

- Reach a relevant shortlist without being overwhelmed.
- Explain why a vehicle fits their needs.
- Identify the leading option's main compromise.
- Recognize important uncertainty or missing evidence.
- Take a concrete next step with greater confidence.
- Avoid unsuitable, misleading, or incorrectly matched offers.

### Ownership outcomes

Drivewise succeeds when owners:

- Keep their vehicle profile current.
- Receive relevant information they would otherwise have missed.
- Understand why an update matters to their specific vehicle.
- Take timely action on safety, maintenance, or regulatory issues.
- Make better-informed maintenance, upgrade, resale, and replacement decisions.
- Continue using Drivewise after the purchase.

### Adaptability outcomes

The advisor succeeds when:

- Beginners understand explanations without needing external research.
- Experienced users can access sufficient technical depth.
- Information is adapted without hiding material risks or uncertainty.
- Users can ask follow-up questions and progressively deepen the analysis.

### Trust outcomes

Trust is the non-negotiable success condition.

Drivewise must maintain:

- Correct vehicle and variant identification.
- Correct offer-to-vehicle pairing.
- Visible and permitted sources.
- Clear dates and freshness indicators.
- Traceable claims and recommendation factors.
- Explicit assumptions, conflicts, and missing information.
- A clear distinction between evidence and inference.
- Zero trust-critical failures in validated user journeys.

A trust-critical failure includes materially incorrect vehicle matching, unauthorized information affecting advice, unsupported provenance, dangerously incomplete guidance, or stale information being presented as current.

### Business and product outcomes

The product demonstrates meaningful value when:

- Users return after the initial purchase decision.
- Ownership notifications lead to useful actions.
- Users report reduced research and comparison effort.
- Continued use reflects trust rather than notification volume.
- The product supports the complete lifecycle without becoming a generic automotive content platform.
- The agent's guidance—not the number of aggregated listings—becomes the primary reason people use Drivewise.
